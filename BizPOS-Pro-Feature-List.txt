==================================================================
BizPOS Pro — Complete Feature List
==================================================================



------------------------------------------------------------------
2. SALES MANAGEMENT
------------------------------------------------------------------
- Create & manage sales orders with full lifecycle
  (pending -> confirmed -> delivered)
- Quotations (estimates) that convert to sales
- Sales Returns with price validation against the original sale
- Invoices, PDF generation, and printing
- Order discounts, shipping charges, and per-line discounts
- Customer-wise sales history


------------------------------------------------------------------
3. PRODUCTS & CATALOG
------------------------------------------------------------------
- Product management with images, pricing, SKU/model, and VAT rates
- Categories, Brands, Units, and Variants (size/color/etc.)
- Barcode label printing
- Bulk import/export
- Selling price, cost price, and stock tracking


------------------------------------------------------------------
4. INVENTORY / STOCK
------------------------------------------------------------------
- Real-time stock overview
- Low-stock alerts
- Stock adjustments (with reasons & history)
- Stock valuation and movement tracking


------------------------------------------------------------------
5. PURCHASING & SUPPLIERS
------------------------------------------------------------------
- Purchase orders with supplier-wise tracking
- Purchase Returns
- Supplier management, supplier groups, and supplier ledgers/balances
- Supplier payments and dues tracking


------------------------------------------------------------------
6. CUSTOMERS (CRM)
------------------------------------------------------------------
- Customer database with profiles & addresses
- Customer Ledger and outstanding balances
- Customer Advances (prepayments)
- Customer Groups and delivery Areas
- Purchase history per customer


------------------------------------------------------------------
7. FINANCE & MONEY
------------------------------------------------------------------
- Cash Flow management and Payment Accounts (cash, bank, mobile money)
- Expenses with categories
- Assets register with categories & depreciation tracking
- Loans management with lenders
- Investment tracking (investors)
- "Today's Summary" / Income views (simple mode for small shops)


------------------------------------------------------------------
8. ACCOUNTING (DOUBLE-ENTRY)
------------------------------------------------------------------
- Journal Entries & General Ledger
- Trial Balance, Profit & Loss, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow Statement
- Credit Notes, Debit Notes, and Receipts
- Auto-balancing journal entries from sales/purchases/payments
- Bangladesh VAT support (Mushak forms, 15% standard NBR rate)


------------------------------------------------------------------
9. ONLINE STORE (ECOMMERCE)
------------------------------------------------------------------
- Full customer-facing storefront with product browsing, cart,
  wishlist, compare
- Checkout with online payment gateways (bKash, Nagad, SSLCommerz)
  and Cash on Delivery
- Customer accounts (register/login, Google sign-in, order history,
  profile)
- Coupons, Flash Deals, Campaigns, Collections
- Banners & drag-and-drop homepage section builder
- Menu builder (admin-managed navigation)
- Blog module
- Shipping Zones & rates
- Contact page (admin-configurable details, banner, map)
- Configurable store theme colors, payment icons, and settings


------------------------------------------------------------------
10. DELIVERY & COURIER INTEGRATION
------------------------------------------------------------------
- Integration with Bangladesh couriers: Pathao, Steadfast, eCourier,
  Redx, Paperfly, Sundarban, SA Paribahan
- Delivery/shipment tracking and courier operations


------------------------------------------------------------------
11. REPORTS & ANALYTICS
------------------------------------------------------------------
- Dashboard with KPIs & charts
- Daily Summary (DTS), Monthly Summary
- Sales reports, Detail Sales, Category-wise sales
- Inventory, Purchase, and Supplier Payment reports
- Receivables Aging, Profit & Loss, Cash Movement
- Customer reports, VAT/Tax reports, Financial reports, Staff reports
- Custom Report Builder
- Excel/PDF export across modules


------------------------------------------------------------------
12. MARKETING
------------------------------------------------------------------
- SMS Campaigns (SSL Wireless, BulkSMSBD gateways)
- Email Marketing
- Loyalty Program (points/rewards)
- Ad Spend tracking (marketing ROI)


------------------------------------------------------------------
13. HR & PAYROLL
------------------------------------------------------------------
- Employee management
- Attendance tracking & reports
- Leave Management (leave types, requests, approvals)
- Weekends & Holidays configuration
- Payroll processing


------------------------------------------------------------------
14. MANUFACTURING (OPTIONAL)
------------------------------------------------------------------
- Production orders & manufacturing dashboard
- Raw materials, raw-material purchases & suppliers
- Colors, Sizes, Factories, Catalogs
- Damages, RM & product wastes
- Reports: RM stock, production, damage, waste, cost analysis


------------------------------------------------------------------
15. AI ASSISTANT
------------------------------------------------------------------
- AI-powered shopping assistant (floating chat widget on the storefront)
- Admin-configurable AI settings




------------------------------------------------------------------
17. SYSTEM, SECURITY & ADMINISTRATION
------------------------------------------------------------------
- User Management with Roles & Permissions
- Backup & Restore
- API Management (keys/tokens for integrations & mobile)
- Multi-location support & Branches
- Printer configuration
- Activity Log & System Logs (full audit trail)
- General settings: business profile, tax/VAT, payment methods,
  courier, invoice/receipt, notifications, localization, SMS gateway
- Simple Mode for small shops (hides advanced accounting/reports)
- Dark mode, multi-language ready, BDT/lakh number formatting
